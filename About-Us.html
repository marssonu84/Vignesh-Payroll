About Vighnesh Payroll Systems

Vighnesh Payroll Systems Software Solutions is our umbrella brand, connecting our education and technology entities through one unified vision for people, institutions and growth.

Under Vighnesh Payroll Systems, Chitra Educational Society represents our education entity and Abhi Software Solutions represents our technology entity. The Vighnesh Payroll Systems platform supports centralised workforce and payroll management across entities and their branches.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide reliable and user-friendly payroll solutions that simplify everyday HR processes, improve accuracy and help businesses save valuable time so they can focus on growth.

Our Vision

To make payroll management simple, smart and accessible for every business by building technology that helps organisations manage their people with confidence and ease.

Founder’s Message

At Vighnesh Payroll Systems, our journey began with a simple vision: to make payroll management easier, faster and more reliable for businesses.

We understand that payroll is not just about numbers. It is about people, trust and timely recognition of every employee’s contribution. Through Vighnesh Payroll Systems, we aim to provide a simple and efficient solution that helps businesses manage payroll with confidence.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to build technology that simplifies processes and empowers organisations to focus on their most valuable asset, their people.

Thank you for being a part of our journey.

Founder, Vighnesh Payroll Systems
