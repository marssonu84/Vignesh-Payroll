VIGHNESH PAYROLL SYSTEMS V10.22 — VISITOR EXPERIENCE EDITION

Purpose
-------
This package provides a public, read-only visitor experience focused on how Vighnesh
Payroll Systems helps organizations manage employees, attendance, payroll workflows,
salary slips, reports, loans/advances, branches and security.

Public-demo principles
----------------------
- Fictional sample data only.
- No production database connection.
- No database writes, edits or deletes.
- No imports, backups/restores, licensing or administration controls.
- Payroll calculation formulas and statutory configuration are intentionally hidden
  from the public visitor experience.
- Detailed payroll calculation methodology can be demonstrated separately during
  administrator/product discussions.

Interactive experience
----------------------
- Employee search/filter and sample profiles.
- Attendance and payroll workflow preview.
- Read-only sample salary slip.
- Reports and statements preview.
- Security and administration overview.
- Guided feature tour.
- Lightweight VFX/motion effects with reduced-motion support.
- Narrated product concept trailer:
  Vighnesh_Visitor_Demo_Concept_Trailer_Narrated.mp4

Entry page
----------
Visitor-Demo.html

The visitor experience is intentionally a product showcase, not a production payroll
application.
