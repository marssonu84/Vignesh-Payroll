Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

At Vighnesh Payroll Systems, we value your trust and are committed to handling information responsibly. This policy explains, in general terms, how information may be handled through the Vighnesh Payroll Systems website and services.

Information we may process

Depending on the features used, information may include account and contact details, employee records, attendance information, salary-related information, bank-related payroll details and other information entered by authorised users.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and operate Vighnesh Payroll Systems features, process user-requested payroll functions, maintain security, troubleshoot issues and improve the service.

Cookies and third-party services

The public website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics and, where enabled, advertising. Third-party services such as Google may process information according to their own policies. Advertising features will only be used where configured on public pages.

Data sharing

Vighnesh Payroll Systems does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the service, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect their account credentials and restrict access appropriately.

User and organisation responsibility

Organisations using Vighnesh Payroll Systems are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority to enter and process employee information and for complying with laws applicable to their use of payroll and personal data.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Vighnesh Payroll Systems features and services develop. The latest version will be published on this page.
