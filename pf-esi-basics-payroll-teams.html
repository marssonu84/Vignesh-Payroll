PF and ESI Basics for Payroll Teams

An introductory workflow for handling PF and ESI-related payroll data without relying on outdated hard-coded assumptions.

Start with applicability

Provident Fund and Employees’ State Insurance are statutory frameworks in India, but applicability depends on current law, establishment circumstances and employee-related conditions. Payroll teams should not assume that every employee is treated identically.

Maintain clean statutory data

Where applicable, keep required identifiers and employee details accurate. Name, date-of-birth or identity mismatches can create administrative problems beyond the monthly salary calculation.

Do not hard-code old rates

Statutory thresholds, contribution rules and administrative procedures can change. Payroll configuration should be reviewed against current official guidance rather than copied indefinitely from an old spreadsheet.

Reconcile payroll and statutory records

The payroll amounts used for statutory processing should reconcile with the organisation’s payroll records. Differences should be investigated before filings or payments are finalised.

Use official sources

For current requirements, employers should consult the Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Employees’ State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), applicable government notifications and qualified payroll or legal professionals.

Vighnesh Payroll Systems approach

A payroll platform can organise statutory fields and calculations, but the employer remains responsible for determining applicability and keeping configuration current. Treat software output as part of a controlled compliance process, not as legal advice.

Important: This article provides general payroll information, not legal, tax or compliance advice. Requirements can change and may differ by establishment and location. Verify current requirements with the relevant government authority or a qualified professional.
